About Us

Lagos-born. Print-obsessed.

Abidexpro Printz started with one mission: bring professional-grade printing to Lagos businesses of every size. From the small entrepreneur getting their first business cards printed to the corporate brand ordering 10,000 flyers — we treat every job the same way.

We run state-of-the-art digital and offset equipment, with in-house finishing (lamination, foiling, binding) so your order stays under one roof from start to finish. No middlemen. No delays.
