About Us

Lagos-born. Print-obsessed.

Abidexpro Printz is a printing company based in Shomolu, Lagos, with one mission: bring professional-grade printing to Lagos businesses of every size. From the small entrepreneur getting their first business cards printed to the corporate brand ordering 10,000 flyers — we treat every job the same way.

We run state-of-the-art digital and offset equipment, with in-house finishing (lamination, foiling, binding) so your order stays under one roof from start to finish. No middlemen. No delays.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does business card printing cost in Lagos?

At Abidexpro Printz, business card printing starts from ₦8,000 for 100 units on premium 350gsm stock. Final price depends on quantity, finish (matte, gloss, foil, spot UV), and turnaround time. Contact us for a custom quote.

Where is Abidexpro Printz located?

We are located at 26, Awofeso, Shomolu, Lagos, Nigeria. You can visit us during business hours (Monday to Saturday, 8am – 7pm) or reach us on WhatsApp for a fast response.

What printing services do you offer?

We offer business cards, flyers and brochures, banners and signage, branded merchandise (T-shirts, mugs, tote bags), packaging and labels, and full design services. Whatever your brand needs printed, we handle it.

How long does printing take?

Most jobs are completed within 2–3 working days. We also offer 48-hour express turnaround for urgent orders like roll-up banners. Turnaround depends on the product and quantity.

Do you offer design services if I don't have artwork?

Yes! Our in-house designers can create print-ready files from scratch at competitive rates. Just tell us your idea and we'll bring it to life before printing.

How do I place an order?

Simply fill the order form on this website or message us directly on WhatsApp. We'll discuss your requirements, send you a quote, and arrange payment and delivery. Fast and easy.
