FAQs

Questions we get every week

Straight answers. Anything not here, just ask us on WhatsApp.

How much does printing cost in Lagos?

Business cards start from ₦15,000 per unit, flyers from ₦45,000 for 500 units, roll-up banners from ₦50,000, and custom t-shirts from ₦6,000 per unit. Final prices depend on quantity, paper, finish and turnaround. Our full pricing guide breaks down what changes the number.

How long will my job take?

Flyers typically take 2 working days, business cards 3, roll-up banners 2 to 3, and custom t-shirts 3 to 5 depending on quantity. Turnaround starts from artwork approval, not from your first message. Express production is available on most items. See our turnaround guide for planning around an event.

Do you offer design, or do I need my own artwork?

Both. If you have print-ready artwork we will print it. If you do not, our design team can create it for you as a separate service. If you only have a logo from WhatsApp or social media, we will usually need to rebuild it at print resolution first.

What file format should I send?

A print-ready PDF is ideal. AI, EPS and high-resolution PNG also work. Please send artwork at 300dpi, in CMYK colour, with 3mm bleed on all edges. Getting this right avoids delays and extra charges.

Is there a minimum order?

It varies by product. Custom t-shirts have a minimum of 10 units. Flyers are quoted from 500 units. Business cards and banners can be ordered in smaller quantities, though the per-unit price is always better at volume.

Do you deliver, and where?

Yes. We deliver across Lagos, and nationwide by courier. Delivery cost depends on location and order size, and is confirmed with your quote. You are also welcome to collect from our shop at 26 Awofeso Street, Shomolu.

Can I see a proof before you print everything?

Always. We send a proof for approval before production begins. Please check spelling, phone numbers and colours carefully at that stage, and reply promptly. A proof waiting unread is the most common cause of a job running late.

How do I pay, and do you need a deposit?

We ask for a deposit before starting, with the balance on completion or delivery. This is standard practice because materials are bought specifically for your job and cannot be reused. Payment details are shared with your quote.

What if there is a problem with my order?

Tell us straight away. If the fault is ours, such as a printing defect or an error against the approved proof, we will reprint it. If artwork was approved with a mistake in it, we will work with you on the best way forward.

Which t-shirt printing method should I choose?

For orders under 20, or any full-colour design, DTF is usually best. For 50 or more shirts with a simple design, screen printing is more economical and more durable. For names and numbers, vinyl. Our t-shirt printing guide compares all four methods.
