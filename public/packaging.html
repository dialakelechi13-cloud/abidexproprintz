Questions

Packaging FAQ

How much does custom packaging cost in Lagos?+
Packaging is quoted per project, because size, material, quantity and finish all move the price significantly. Send your product dimensions and target quantity and we will come back with a firm number.
What is the minimum order for custom boxes?+
It varies by format. Some styles are viable from a few hundred units, others need more to be economical. Tell us your quantity and we will tell you honestly whether it works or whether a different format suits you better.
Is your packaging food safe?+
We offer food-safe options for bakery, takeaway and food brands. Tell us what the packaging will hold and we will specify the right material.
Can you design the packaging artwork?+
Yes. Packaging artwork has to be laid out on a die line, which is different from designing a flyer. Our team handles this, or we can supply the die line if your designer prefers to work from it.
How long does packaging production take?+
Typically 5 to 10 working days from artwork approval, depending on the format and quantity. Rigid and specialist boxes take longer than printed mailers or bags.
