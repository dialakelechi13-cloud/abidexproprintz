Questions

Sticker and label FAQ

How much does sticker printing cost in Lagos?+
Sticker and label pricing depends on size, material, shape and quantity, and the per-unit cost falls significantly with volume. Send us your size and target quantity and we will quote it exactly.
Can you cut stickers to a custom shape?+
Yes. We die-cut to your outline, so the sticker can follow the shape of your logo rather than sitting inside a square. Send vector artwork where possible for the cleanest cut.
Are your stickers waterproof?+
Vinyl stickers are. If your label is going on a bottle, a jar, a cooler or anything outdoors, we will recommend vinyl. Paper labels are cheaper but are for dry surfaces only.
What file format should I send?+
Vector files are best, such as AI, EPS, PDF or SVG, especially for die-cut shapes. A high resolution PNG with a transparent background also works for most jobs.
How long does sticker printing take?+
Most sticker and label orders are ready within 2 to 3 working days from artwork approval. Large or specialist runs may take longer.
