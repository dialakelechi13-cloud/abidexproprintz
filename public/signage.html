Questions

Signage FAQ

How much does shop signage cost in Lagos?+
Every sign is a different size, material and mounting job, so we quote each one individually rather than publishing a flat rate. Send a photo of the space and rough measurements and we will come back with a firm price, free and with no obligation.
Do you install the signage or just make it?+
Both. We install across Lagos, including mounting at height where the site allows. If you have your own fitter, we can supply the sign ready to install.
How long does signage take?+
Most signage takes 4 to 7 working days from approval, depending on the material and whether fabrication is involved. Illuminated and 3D letter signs take longer than flat printed boards.
Will the sign fade in the sun?+
Not quickly, if it is made properly. We use UV-stable inks and outdoor-grade substrates. Cheap indoor materials used outdoors are the usual reason signs fade within months.
Can you match my existing branding?+
Yes. Send your logo and brand colours and we will match them. If you only have a low resolution logo, we will rebuild it at the size needed.
