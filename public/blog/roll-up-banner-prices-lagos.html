Roll-Up Banner Prices in Lagos: What You Should Actually Pay

6 August 2026 · 6 min read · Abidexpro Printz, Shomolu Lagos

Roll-up banners are the most common thing Lagos businesses order badly. Not because they pick the wrong printer, but because they compare on price alone — and with banners, price alone tells you almost nothing.

What a roll-up banner should cost

A standard 2400mm × 800mm roll-up banner in Lagos starts from around ₦50,000, including the stand and a carry bag. That is for premium banner material with a stand you can use repeatedly.

You will find them advertised for considerably less. The saving almost always comes from one of three places: thinner print material that shows light through it, a lower resolution print that looks soft up close, or a stand mechanism that jams or leans.

The honest test: a banner is not a print, it is a piece of equipment with a print attached. Ask specifically about the stand before you compare prices.

Understanding the material

The two things that matter:

  • Opacity. Cheap banner material is translucent. Stand it in front of a window or under strong event lighting and your design goes muddy. Blockout material has a grey backing layer that stops light passing through.
  • Curl resistance. Thin material curls at the edges after a few roll-ups. Once it curls, it never sits flat again.

Understanding the stand

This is where most of the real price difference lives. A good stand has a smooth retraction mechanism, a base with enough weight to stay upright in a breezy hall, and a pole that locks properly rather than relying on friction.

A banner used once a year can get away with a basic stand. A banner that travels to a different event every month cannot — and replacing it twice costs more than buying properly once.

The design mistakes that waste banners

Putting important content at the bottom

The bottom 400mm of a roll-up is frequently hidden behind a table, a chair, or a person standing in front of it. Anything below that line is decoration, not communication. Keep your logo, headline and call to action in the top two thirds.

Too many words

People read banners while walking past. You have a few seconds. One clear headline, three short supporting points at most, and a way to contact you. Every extra sentence reduces the odds any of it gets read.

Low resolution artwork

A banner is printed at roughly life size. A logo that looks fine on a business card can look badly pixelated when blown up to 800mm wide. Send vector artwork where possible — AI, EPS, PDF or SVG.

Forgetting the phone number

It sounds obvious. It happens constantly. Put a phone number or WhatsApp on the banner, large enough to read from three metres.

How long they take

Standard turnaround for a roll-up banner in Lagos is typically two to three working days from artwork approval. Express 48-hour production is usually available at a premium. More detail in our turnaround guide.

Buying for a specific event

Order at least a week before the event, not two days. This is not a printer being awkward — it is because if something goes wrong with the artwork, the material or the stand, a week gives room to fix it and two days does not. Events do not move.

If you need several banners for a multi-stand event, ask about bulk pricing. Producing four at once is significantly cheaper per unit than four separate jobs, for the same reason covered in our general printing costs guide.

Common questions

How much is a roll-up banner in Lagos?

From about 50,000 naira for a standard 2400 by 800mm banner in premium material, including the stand and carry bag. Larger sizes and bulk orders are priced differently.

How long does a roll-up banner last?

With a good stand and careful rolling, several years of regular use. Cheap material typically starts curling at the edges within months.

What file format should I send for a banner?

Vector formats are best: AI, EPS, PDF or SVG. If sending a raster image, it should be high resolution at or near full banner size, in CMYK.

Can I reuse the stand and just reprint the banner?

Yes, on most quality stands the cartridge can be replaced. This is one of the practical reasons to buy a decent stand initially.
